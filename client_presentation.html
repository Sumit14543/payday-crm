Operational Roadmap

Lending Pipeline & Role Allocation

Every operational step, from lead submission to disbursal and repayment collections, is assigned to dedicated user roles to ensure fast SLAs, absolute regulatory compliance, and a seamless customer experience.

Admin: Configures lending limits, manages API integrations, and supervises system yields.
Telecaller: Engages prospects, registers callback notes, and requests bank statements.
Credit Manager: Fetches CIBIL, triggers eKYC, approves terms, and issues sanction agreements.
Accountant: Audits beneficiary details, calculates payouts net of GST, and enters bank UTR.
Collection: Tracks overdue cases, registers partial repayments, and logs PTP schedules.

Step-by-Step Lending Roadmap

1
Lead Ingestion & Form Fill Customer

Borrower submits the online lead form, automatically initializing the prospect dossier with tracking parameters.

2
Verification & Document Collection Telecaller

Telecaller validates contact info, updates callback remarks, and dispatches a dynamic document upload link to the borrower.

3
Risk Appraisal & eKYC Verification Credit Manager

Credit Manager executes bureau credit checks, verifies Aadhaar eKYC via dynamic OTP, configures ROI rates, and issues the sanction sheet.

4
Agreement Signing (eSign) Customer

Customer views the dynamic NBFC contract and signs it using remote Aadhaar-linked digital signature gateways.

5
Disbursal Check & Net Calculations Accountant

Accountant validates beneficiary bank accounts, automatically nets platform processing fees and GST, and processes the transfer.

6
UTR Binding & Schedule Activation Accountant

Accountant binds the unique bank transfer UTR number to activate the interest schedule and dynamic EMI repayment cycles.

7
Overdue Follow-up & Collections Collection Executive

Collection executive monitors overdue buckets (DPD 1-3, DPD 4-7, NPA), logs repayment PTPs, and reconciles payments via Cashfree links.
